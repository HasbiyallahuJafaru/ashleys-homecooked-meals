# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router, TypeScript), Tailwind v4, Motion. Chosen by the user ("build it in nextjs").

## Users

People in and around Tacoma, Washington who want a homemade soul food plate for themselves or their
family, usually arriving from Facebook, Instagram, TikTok or word of mouth, mostly on a phone. Also
people looking for someone to cater an event. Their job: see what Ashley cooks, what it costs, and
how to get an order in.

## Product Purpose

Marketing site for Ashley's Homecooked Meals LLC, a new small business (since 2025) serving homemade
soul food. The site replaces a basic Webador site. Success is a visitor texting or calling Ashley to
place a pre-order or a catering enquiry.

## Positioning

One cook, Ashley, making soul food to order: nothing sits under a heat lamp. Everything is
pre-ordered at least 24 hours ahead and cooked for that customer. Her line: "Let me feed you."

## Operating Context

- Pre-orders only, placed at least 24 hours ahead. Payment due at ordering. No refunds.
- Curbside pickup only; pickup location and time are sent the day before. Delivery may be arranged
  by text, is not guaranteed, and cost varies by location.
- Open Thursdays, Fridays and Sundays. Times vary.
- Payment by Cash App ($Ashleyshomecookedmealsllc) or Zelle (253-384-9014).
- Orders and questions go by text or call to (206) 476-0691, or email Businessashley90@gmail.com.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- MVP is showcase only: menu with prices plus text/call actions. No cart, checkout, form or backend.
- Single scrolling page.
- Sides, a side of wings and extra catfish fillets are add-ons to meals only.
- Allergen notice: items may contain shellfish, dairy, eggs, nuts and gluten.
- Undecided / inconsistent in the source: the Oxtail plate is $40 in the site text and $35 on both
  menu flyers; the "Big bacc combo" is spelled "Big Back" on the flyer; catering has no packages or
  pricing; opening times are not stated.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Ashley's Homecooked Meals LLC. "Since 2025."
- Pinned by the user: white background, orange accents taken from the logo (changed from gold at the user's request); premium and rich, with a homemade feel;
  animated transitions between sections; image frames are circular.
- Voice from the source: warm, plain, first person. "Homemade soulful meals made with love."
  "Let me feed you." "Cooked with love, just for you."
- Named house drink: Ashley's Soul Punch.

## Evidence on Hand

- Six real phone photos in `public/images/`: collard greens, two fried catfish and wings baskets,
  baked mac and cheese, strawberry banana pudding tray, hamburger steak plate with greens and mac.
- Promo flyers (text baked in, illustrative food renders): full menu, oxtail menu, catfish and wings
  promo, branded T-shirt image. Used as the source of menu facts, not as photography.
- Logo (orange square with chef hat and crossed knives) and a business card photo.
- One testimonial: "The food from Ashley's Homecooked Meals is absolutely divine!" from a Tacoma customer.
- Absent, do not fabricate: owner biography, more testimonials, dish photos for oxtail, pastas,
  salmon, shrimp, wing bowls; catering packages; press.

## Product Principles

1. The food is the proof. Show real plates, state real prices.
2. One clear action: text Ashley to order.
3. Say the rules up front (pre-order, 24 hours, pickup) so nobody is surprised.
4. Never claim what the business has not said.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, visible focus, reduced-motion support. Phone-first.
